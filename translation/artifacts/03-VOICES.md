# Voice Cards and the Voice Ledger

The game shows a **portrait, never a name**, so the player identifies a
speaker by voice alone. That raises, not lowers, the cost of drift.

Speakers are keyed by the three-digit voice index inside the record id (the
last three characters). One character often owns several indices -- different
recording sessions, not different people. The mapping below was established by
self-identification in the script (a character naming themselves or being
named by the person answering them), not by guesswork; where it rests on
inference the row says so.

## Voice Cards

```
EMMA GRANGER . Lt., flight control / navigator . 20s . defers to the bridge;
               the player defers to her in the air and outranks her in nothing
SOURCE MARKERS: consistently polite-neutral, softens with the player over the
               game, uses trailing pauses where she will not finish a thought
ENGLISH VOICE: warm-professional. Medium sentences. Contractions yes.
               Anglo-Saxon vocabulary, no jargon she has not been given.
               Profanity ceiling: none. Never raises her voice except for a
               missile.
LEXICAL BANDS: navigation and care -- "watch your", "stay with", "come back".
               Banned: slang, sarcasm, any word Focker would use.
ARC:           Phase 1 instructor ("Second Lieutenant", full titles).
               Phase 2 controller (rank title, warmer).
               Phase 3 (final mission) drops the title and does not finish
               her sentences.
ANCHOR LINES:  "Welcome to the U.N. Spacy Valkyrie Corps. I'm Lt. Emma
                Granger, and I'll be your navigator."
               "Watch your altitude -- there are civilians down there."
               "Apollo Leader... come back safe. Please."
NOT:           Bridge Control -- Emma addresses the player, Control addresses
               the formation. Not the junior operator: Emma never panics.
```

```
ROY FOCKER . Major, Skull Leader . 30s . answers to Global; everyone else
             answers to him
SOURCE MARKERS: rough-male register throughout, plural mockery for trainees,
             barking imperatives, drops politeness even upward
ENGLISH VOICE: loud, short, physical. Sentences under twelve words.
             Contractions always. Anglo-Saxon only -- he does not have a
             Latinate word in him. Profanity ceiling: "damn", used twice.
LEXICAL BANDS: flight and drinking and bar-room. Banned: any procedural
             phrasing, any hedge, the word "please".
ARC:           Unchanging until the death scene, where the volume drops and
             the sentences get shorter still. That is the whole effect --
             do not add tenderness he does not have.
ANCHOR LINES:  "All right, rookies -- welcome aboard the Prometheus."
               "Don't get cocky!"
               "Purple Leader. From here on it's you and Hikaru carrying
                this outfit... I'm counting on you."
NOT:           Kamjin, who is also loud. Focker is loud *at* people he intends
             to keep alive; Kamjin is loud at people he is spending.
```

```
BRIDGE CONTROL . Gunsight 1 / Delta 1 . the Macross's fighter direction
SOURCE MARKERS: clipped operational Japanese, imperative forms, occasional
             feminine sentence-final softening under stress
ENGLISH VOICE: procedural. Verb-first. Six to twelve words. No contractions
             in an order, contractions when the situation slips. Latinate
             where the navy is Latinate ("intercept", "engage", "evacuate").
LEXICAL BANDS: gunnery, damage control, evacuation. Banned: encouragement,
             names, jokes.
ARC:           None. Composure is the point.
ANCHOR LINES:  "This is Gunsight 1. All Valkyries, scramble."
               "Macross damage: 40 percent."
               "Missiles are prohibited -- you will bring the city down."
NOT:           Emma, who is talking to one person. Control is talking to a
             formation and will not say "you" if it can say a callsign.
```

```
JUNIOR OPERATOR . the bridge's youngest . composure is her arc and she has
             none of it
SOURCE MARKERS: drawn-out sentence endings, whines, near-tears under fire
ENGLISH VOICE: half a step out of procedure. Sentences trail. She reaches for
             the right phrase, misses, and says the human one.
LEXICAL BANDS: the same vocabulary as Control, misapplied. Banned: any
             correctly-formed order.
ANCHOR LINES:  "We can't take much more of this...!"
               "Somebody do something!"
               "Purple Squadron -- please -- that's outside your sector..."
NOT:           Bridge Control. If the line is correct, it is not hers.
```

```
EDDIE JUUTILAINEN . Sgt., wing 2 . the player's mouth
SOURCE MARKERS: crude-casual male, drops syllables, refers to himself
             grandly in the third person once
ENGLISH VOICE: fast, loud, contracted, wrong. Sentences under eight words.
             Profanity ceiling: "damn it".
LEXICAL BANDS: bragging, scoring, girls, food. Banned: correct procedure,
             any sentence with a subordinate clause.
ARC:           None. He dies as he lived, mid-boast.
ANCHOR LINES:  "Leave it to Eddie!"
               "The hell was that?!"
               "Ha! If I go down here you'll pass my score--"
NOT:           Bruce. Eddie says the thing; Bruce says whether it was true.
```

```
BRUCE RUDEL . Sgt., wing 3 . the player's second thought
SOURCE MARKERS: plain-male, measured, completes Eddie's sentences correctly
ENGLISH VOICE: dry, level, a beat slower. Ten to fifteen words. Contractions
             yes, exclamation marks rarely.
LEXICAL BANDS: assessment, odds, weather, machinery. Banned: bragging,
             panic, the word "cool".
ANCHOR LINES:  "Alien salvage. Can't really complain."
               "Easy. Getting worked up is how you die out here."
               "Apollo 3. I'm hit -- I've lost control."
NOT:           Eddie, obviously. Also not Bridge Control: Bruce narrates,
             Control directs.
```

```
CAPTAIN GLOBAL (Global Report narrator) . the game's historian
SOURCE MARKERS: written register, past tense, first person, formal but not
             stiff; occasional dry irony about his own decisions
ENGLISH VOICE: a despatch. Long sentences allowed -- the only voice where
             they are. Latinate freely. No contractions. Never exclaims.
LEXICAL BANDS: command, judgement, consequence. Banned: slang, rhetorical
             questions, present tense.
ARC:           Confidence in chapter 1; by the Earth chapters he is
             admitting error in plain words. Do not soften that.
ANCHOR LINES:  "In 1999, an object came without warning from space: a
                colossal battleship 1,200 metres long."
               "That was my error of judgement..."
               "Only the Macross, at the centre of it, was spared. There is
                no word for that but irony."
NOT:           The other Global Report narrators (see below).
```

**The Global Report has six narrators, not one.** The block is a set of
alternate commentary tracks and the voice must switch cleanly:

| **A civilian diarist** | 2605-2621, 2649-2651, 2671-2674 | present perfect, polite, warm; school, the town, "everyone" | the only track that mentions high school. Row 2651 is unmistakably Minmay; row 2618 names Minmay in the third person. Both persons are kept as written -- see Q-13 |

**Frequency policy.** Japanese uses a callsign where English uses nothing.
Radio traffic keeps a callsign only when (a) the line opens a transmission,
(b) the addressee changes, or (c) the line is an order to one unit among
several. Everywhere else it is dropped. Applied blind, the callsigns turn
every mission into a roll call, which is the loudest possible tell of
unedited output.

## Quirk Register

| Quirk | Source marker | English carrier | Frequency | Position | Suspended when | Approved example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focker's mockery of trainees | plural diminutive for the rookies | the word **"rookie(s)"** | ~1 line in 3 of his | vocative, front | when he is hit, and in the death scene | "All right, rookies, you heard it." |
| Eddie's swagger | self-aggrandising third person; dropped syllables | third-person self-reference, exactly **once**, plus dropped auxiliaries | once for the third person; dropped auxiliaries ~1 in 3 | anywhere | when a squadmate is hit | "Leave it to Eddie!" |
| Junior operator's wobble | drawn-out sentence ending | a sentence that **trails into "..."** or breaks and restarts | ~1 line in 2 of hers | end | never -- it is all she does | "Barrier output falling! We can't-- it won't hold!" |
| Zentradi formality | archaic-authoritative register | **no contractions, ever**, plus a "shall/must" modal | every line of Exsedol/Britai/Bodolza | throughout | never | "We shall not be permitted to remain." |
| Kamjin's contempt | rough imperatives, spends his own men | orders that name **no one** -- "you lot", "the rest of you" | ~1 in 2 | front | never | "You lot -- jam them. Make it loud!" |
| Kakizaki's third person | names himself as subject | **"Kakizaki"** as his own subject | twice, both in the Global Report | subject | in battle, where he is too busy | "Leave it to Kakizaki." |
| Zentradi transmission | source wraps enemy comms in corner brackets | **[square brackets]** around the whole line | every enemy transmission heard by the player | whole line | when the speaker is on the Macross's own channel after the ceasefire | "[Micronian weapons! How did the enemy get aboard?]" |

**Uniqueness check:** no two carriers shared. Trailing "..." belongs to the
junior operator *and* to Emma's phase 3 -- resolved by position: Emma's
trailing occurs only at the end of a *complete* thought she declines to
finish; the operator's occurs mid-sentence.
