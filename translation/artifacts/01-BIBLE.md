# The Bible (canon)

What the script assumes and never explains. Built from the source during the
pass; every entry earned its place by resolving an actual line.

No Japanese source text (C7).

---

## Synopsis

1999. A ship falls out of the sky onto South Ataria Island. Earth unifies to
rebuild it. Ten years later, on the day the rebuilt ship -- **SDF-1 Macross**
-- is commissioned, her main gun fires itself at an approaching fleet, and
the aliens fire back. The Macross makes an untested space fold to escape,
lands in Pluto orbit with fifty-six thousand civilians and two carriers
welded to her hull, and spends the rest of the game walking home.

The player is a **new pilot who reports aboard on the morning of that
commissioning**, flies as Skull 7 under Roy Focker, is promoted to lead his
own flight (Purple, or Apollo), and finishes the war inside the enemy flagship.

## Factions and forces

**U.N. Spacy / U.N. Government.** Earth's unified military, formed to rebuild
the fallen ship; won the Unification War against the Anti-U.N. forces. Its
headquarters is under Alaska and its last weapon is the **Grand Cannon**.
Chain of command in this game: HQ Earth -> Captain Global -> Col. Maistroff
(exec) -> squadron leaders -> the player.

**The Macross herself is a character.** 1,200 m; carries a city of ~56,000
civilians inside her, two warships bolted to her flanks (the carrier
**Prometheus**, the assault ship **Daedalus**), and a main gun that only fires
if she folds her whole hull into a humanoid shape -- **Transformation** --
which wrecks the city inside her every time she does it. Later refits let the
city evacuate first. Her defensive **Pin Point Barrier** is an accident of the
vanished fold system, and its overload later erases a fifty-kilometre circle
of Earth.

**Zentradi.** A species of giants, cloned rather than born, whose entire
society is its army. Men and women are segregated absolutely; the sight of
both aboard one ship is a scandal. They can shrink volunteers to human size
-- **Micloneation** -- and they have fought a war they no longer remember the
start of. They fear that humanity is **Protoculture**: their makers.
Britai's fleet fights the Macross, then allies with her. Bodolza's Grand
Fleet -- 4,791,122 ships -- comes to erase both.

**Meltrandi.** The Zentradi's female counterpart and enemy, commanded by
Laplamiz. Milia Fallyna is their ace.

**Protoculture.** The ancient civilisation that split itself into Zentradi and
Meltrandi and seeded Earth. Its surviving weapon is culture itself: a song
disarms a Zentradi soldier where a missile does not.

## Relationship map

- **Roy Focker** commands Skull; he is the player's first CO and dies about
  three-quarters through. **Claudia LaSalle** is his partner -- she never says
  so in a briefing, only in the trading-card scenes, where she calls him
  "Roy" and no one else does.
- **Hikaru Ichijo** leads Vermilion; **Hayao Kakizaki** and **Maximilian
  Jenius** fly with him. Max inherits Skull after Focker. Kakizaki dies in the
  barrier overload.
- **Misa Hayase** is Gunsight 1's controller, is captured with Hikaru and
  Focker at Saturn, returns with the intelligence that changes the war, and is
  promoted from First Lieutenant to Captain in the process.
- **Lynn Minmay** wins Miss Macross, becomes an idol, and becomes the weapon.
  **Lynn Kaifun** is her manager and a source of pressure on Misa.
- **Milia Fallyna** attacks the Macross to kill its best pilot, loses, infiltrates
  in Micronian form to finish the job, and marries Max instead.
- **Emma Granger** is the player's navigator from the training course to the
  last mission -- the game's own invention, and its emotional throughline.
- **Eddie Juutilainen** and **Bruce Rudel** are assigned to the player at
  promotion and fly as 2 and 3 until they die.

## Motifs and running gags

- **"Rookie."** Focker's word for the player, first line to last. It is how
  the player is greeted (row 229) and it is the last thing Focker's voice does
  in a tutorial hint (row 1812).
- **Trading cards.** Every promotion and every rank threshold produces someone
  pressing a Valkyrie trading card on the player. It is the game's way of
  saying *this is a world with a merchandise industry inside a war*, and it is
  also how Claudia grieves.
- **Pineapple salad.** Focker's promised meal; his last line before he dies is
  about it. Do not explain it in the text; the source doesn't.
- **"Culture."** The Zentradi word for everything they lack. It arrives as
  scorn, becomes curiosity, and ends as a battle cry.
- **Altitude, area, missiles.** Three nagging constraints the controllers
  repeat all game. They are the source's own repetition and must stay
  repetitive -- but see the Voice Ledger's variation policy.

## Open canon questions (deliberate -- do not "fix")

1. **The player has no lines and no name.** They are addressed constantly and
   never speak. Every reply is another character's. Keep it that way.
2. **The player's gender is never marked** in any line addressed to them.
   English must keep it unmarked: no "sir", no "he", no "young man".
3. **Emma's feelings are never stated.** They are audible in a hesitation and
   an unfinished sentence and in Eddie's whistle. They stay unstated.
4. **Whether the Macross's opening shot was a trap** is Global's private
   theory, not a fact, in every place he says it.
